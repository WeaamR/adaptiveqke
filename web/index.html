WHY TWO LATENCIES?

Wire latency stays outside the constrained client.

The byte-counting proxy measures from the first client→server bytes to wire quiescence, reducing observer throttling. Real client scheduling delays still affect traffic. See the documented measurement limitations.

WHAT DOES ADAPTIVE OPTIMIZE?

A policy balance, not minimum latency alone.

Under favorable conditions the policy may retain the strongest evaluated group even if lighter static groups have lower latency.
